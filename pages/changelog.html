What’s new in Delivrie

RSS feedIn the docs
Nov 27, 2026

Prices in US dollars for everyone

Billing
  • Every plan, top-up and overage is now priced and paid in US dollars by card through Stripe, wherever you are.
Nov 23, 2026

Audit logs

SecurityAPI
  • Enterprise teams can see who changed what in Settings -> Audit log: sign-ins, members and roles, invites, API keys, domains, webhooks, single sign-on, connected apps, billing and published content.
  • Entries can't be edited or deleted and can be exported as CSV. Also available as GET /audit-logs.
  • Activity is recorded for every team, so the full history is there on upgrade.
Nov 23, 2026

Starter daily limit

Plans
  • The Starter plan now has a daily limit of 1,500 emails (20,000 a month is unchanged). Sends past it return 429 daily_quota_exceeded until midnight UTC.
  • Pro and Scale keep no daily limit.
Nov 23, 2026

One-click DNS setup on Cloudflare

DomainsAPI
  • Domains on Cloudflare can now be set up in one step: open the domain, choose Set up with Cloudflare, and paste a Cloudflare token with Zone: Read and DNS: Edit.
  • Delivrie only adds missing records. Existing SPF, DMARC and MX records are never changed, so your current email keeps working.
  • The token is used once and never stored. Also available as POST /domains/:id/cloudflare.
Nov 20, 2026

Forward received email to another inbox

ReceivingAPI
  • Read your Delivrie mail in Gmail, Outlook or any inbox: turn on Forward a copy on a domain's Receiving page and every received email is also sent to up to 5 addresses.
  • Copies come from the address that received the email and reply to the original sender, with attachments up to 10 MB.
  • Also available in the API and MCP server as receiving_forward_to on PATCH /domains/:id.
Nov 18, 2026

Dollar payments move to Stripe

Billing
  • Pay in US dollars by card through Stripe, with a receipt for every payment and sales tax or VAT worked out by Stripe Tax.
  • Update your card, download receipts and edit your billing address and tax ID in the billing portal under Billing → Payment methods.
  • Plan changes on a dollar plan take effect straight away: the difference is charged or credited for the rest of the period.
Nov 14, 2026

30 days of email history on every plan

DashboardAPI
  • Email content, sent and received, is now kept for 30 days on every plan, up from 1 to 7 days on Free, Starter, Pro and Scale.
  • After 30 days the body and attachments are deleted; recipients, status, events and Inbox threads stay.
Nov 14, 2026

Official Node.js SDK

APISDK
  • npm install delivrie: a typed client for every endpoint, with no dependencies. Runs on Node.js 18+, Bun, Deno, Cloudflare Workers and Vercel Edge.
  • Calls return { data, error } instead of throwing, and sends are only retried with an idempotency key, so a retry never sends twice.
  • verifyWebhook checks webhook signatures in one line. Guide at /docs/sdks.
Nov 14, 2026

New Starter plan and a bigger Pro

Billing
  • Starter is $10 a month for 20,000 emails, the plan Pro used to be.
  • Pro now includes 60,000 emails a month for $20. Existing Pro subscriptions move to the new Pro, with the new price from their next renewal.
  • Scale and pay as you go are unchanged.
Nov 13, 2026

SMTP relay on ports 587 and 465

SMTPSending
  • Send from WordPress, Laravel, Django, Ghost, Supabase Auth and anything else that speaks SMTP: host smtp.delivrie.io, username delivrie, an API key as the password.
  • Port 587 with STARTTLS and port 465 with SSL/TLS, plus 2587 and 2465 for hosts that block the usual ports.
  • SMTP mail gets the same limits, logs, webhooks and suppressions as the API. Tag messages with an X-Delivrie-Tags header.
Nov 13, 2026

Support chat

Dashboard
  • Chat with the Delivrie team from any page in the dashboard. Replies arrive in the chat and by email.
Nov 13, 2026

Automations and Inbox

AutomationsInboxAPI
  • Automations send emails when an event arrives, a contact joins a segment, or on a schedule, with waits, conditions and branches.
  • Custom events API: POST /api/events with an event name, email and data.
  • Inbox: received email grouped into threads, with labels, archive and replies that keep Message-ID threading.
  • Runs show every step with timestamps; retry or skip a failed step after fixing the cause.

Docs, legal pages and client logos

DashboardDocs

  • Official logos for Claude, Cursor, v0, Codex, Antigravity and VS Code in the Works with sections.

New pricing, credits and billing

Billing

  • Paid plans keep sending past their allowance and pay overage per 1,000 at month end. Set a spending cap in Billing.
  • Pay-as-you-go credits from $5 that never expire, and a marketing add-on priced by contacts.

Test addresses and natural-language scheduling

API

  • scheduled_at accepts phrases like “in 1 hour” and “tomorrow at 9am”.
